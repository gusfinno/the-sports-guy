How to use THESPORTSGUY

Welcome to THESPORTSGUY, a website giving you in-depth information on your favorite sports!

You are able to access different sports through the navbar at the top of the page where clicking on the name of the sport will take you to the appropriate page.
Clicking on the name of the website itself will return you to this page.
Once on individual pages, individual events such as races or games can be viewed in depth by clicking anywhere within its box

Which look like this.

For sports with various ladders such as F1 which has both a drivers and constructors (team) championship, the ladders can be switched by hovering the name of the ladder and selecting the desired option.
The tables within each specific event overview can be manipulated to reveal more information by clicking the row where an arrow icon appears which looks like this ▸

If you are placed on an error page, feel free to exit by clicking the navigators in the navbar and continuing on the website.
